How-To Guides

Using an AI assistant well takes more than asking it something. What matters is what you do before, during, and after.

What These Guides Cover

Designed to tackle one essential task at a time, each guide takes less than two minutes to read and follows a simple blueprint:

  • The Outcome — What will be done by the end.
  • The Steps — A clear, practical walkthrough.
  • The Example — What it looks like in a real situation.
  • The Test — A quick way to check that it worked.
