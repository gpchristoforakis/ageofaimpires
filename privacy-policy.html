Privacy Policy

This notice explains what personal data AGE OF AIMPIRES may process, why it is processed, who may receive it, and the rights available to you under EU data-protection law.

Effective date: 29 September 2026

1. Who Is Responsible For Your Data

The data controller for this website is George Paris Christoforakis, publishing as AGE OF AIMPIRES, based in Nicosia, Cyprus.

Privacy contact: hello@ageofaimpires.com

2. What Data We Process

AGE OF AIMPIRES is designed to collect as little personal data as reasonably possible.

When You Contact Us

If you contact us by email or through the contact form, we process the information you provide, including your email address and message and, if you choose to provide it, your phone or WhatsApp number.

Technical And Security Data

When you visit the site, our hosting and security providers may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the website. This can include IP address, request and device information, timestamps, and security-related logs.

Comment Forms

The comment boxes currently shown on some article pages are preview-only and do not submit comments or personal data to a server. If public comment submission or a newsletter signup is enabled later, this policy will be updated before personal data is collected through those features.

3. Why We Process Data

  • To respond to messages and enquiries. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in communicating with readers and responding to requests. Where a message relates to steps you ask us to take before entering into an agreement, processing may also be necessary for those pre-contractual steps.
  • To operate, secure and troubleshoot the website. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in maintaining a reliable and secure publication.
  • To comply with legal obligations or establish, exercise or defend legal claims where required.

Where we rely on consent for any future optional feature, you may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

4. Cookies And Analytics

As of the effective date above, AGE OF AIMPIRES does not use non-essential analytics, advertising, or behavioural-tracking cookies in its own site code.

Our hosting or security infrastructure may use strictly necessary technologies to deliver or protect the service. These do not require consent where they are genuinely necessary for the service requested.

If we introduce analytics, advertising, social-tracking, or other non-essential cookies or similar technologies, they will not be activated before the required consent is obtained, and this policy will be updated.

5. Who Receives Personal Data

We do not sell or rent personal data. The website is hosted and protected using Cloudflare, and contact-form messages are transmitted using Resend as our email-delivery provider. These providers may process personal data only to the extent needed to provide their services to us.

We may also disclose information where required by law, court order, or a competent authority.

6. International Transfers

Some service providers may process data outside Cyprus or the European Economic Area. Where the GDPR requires safeguards for such transfers, we rely on an applicable adequacy decision or other lawful safeguards such as approved standard contractual clauses.

7. How Long We Keep Data

  • Contact messages: kept only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain appropriate correspondence records, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations. When they are no longer needed, they are deleted or anonymised where appropriate.
  • Technical and security logs: retained only for the period reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and service operation, including the retention periods applied by our hosting and security providers.

Data may be deleted earlier when it is no longer needed.

8. Your Rights

Subject to the conditions in the GDPR, you may have the right to:

  • access your personal data;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • request erasure of your data;
  • request restriction of processing;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • receive certain data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
  • lodge a complaint with a data-protection supervisory authority.

To exercise your rights, email hello@ageofaimpires.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

9. Complaints

If you believe your personal data has been handled unlawfully, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Cyprus. You may also have the right to complain to another competent EU/EEA supervisory authority depending on your circumstances.

10. Third-Party Links

The site contains links to external websites and services. Once you follow an external link, that third party controls its own data practices. We encourage you to review the privacy information provided by that service.

11. Children

AGE OF AIMPIRES is a general-audience publication and is not designed to collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, contact us so we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

12. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the site, our data practices, or applicable requirements change. The effective date shown above will be updated when material changes are made.
