Age of AI Empires is an independent publication about what AI changes when it meets actual work.

It is for people who do not need another breathless prediction about the future. They need to know whether a tool saves time, creates more work, improves a decision, or quietly turns a small assignment into an afternoon.

The publication starts with accessible essays and keeps a more rigorous layer underneath: frameworks, definitions, test protocols, and working notes. The aim is credible thinking, not academic theatre.

The first series examines the economics of AI-assisted work: direct price, hidden effort, scope expansion, completion boundaries, and the difference between task cost and task value.

**Written and edited by George Christoforakis.**
